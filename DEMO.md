# HELIOS·LUNA demo script (about 3 minutes)

Open the live link. Nothing needs explaining before the first click.

1. **Compare (0:00).** "Ten Artemis III candidate sites, one touchdown date. Each card says GO, CHECK or NO-GO against
   the mission profile, and why." Point at a NO-GO reason ("sunlit 12% < 80%").
2. **Mission profile (0:20).** Open it, pick *Artemis III-like crew stay*. "These criteria come from NASA's site-selection
   documents: continuous sunlight for the stay, Earth in view." Grades update instantly.
3. **Schedule slip (0:40).** In the date matrix, click a later column. "Launch slipped two weeks: here is every site on
   the new date." Switch to **Site**, press *Save current as Plan A*, move the touchdown date, read the change column:
   "link −4 points, first sunset 5 days sooner."
4. **Windows (1:20).** "When can we land at all?" Every touchdown hour with the Sun at a usable camera angle, a ground
   station in view through descent, and a stay that works. *Add to calendar* drops them into Outlook.
5. **Site (1:50).** The real horizon from 300 km of LOLA terrain, the Sun and Earth at true size, Earth's phase. The
   operations clock: "sunset in 3 d 23 h, Earthset in 11 d, next contact in 1 h 48 min." Landing zone: 100 m, slope under
   10° across the whole zone.
6. **Map (2:20).** Every pixel ray-traced to the Sun on the curved Moon; press play and the shadows sweep the pole. Zoom
   to 20 m terrain at Malapert.
7. **Trust (2:40).** *⋯ → Methods & validation*: twelve automated tests, including JPL Horizons (0.00012°), SPICE, NASA PGDA maps and LROC
   images. *Export → Horizon mask* hands the exact horizon to STK.

Closing line: "From a slipped date to a new landing plan, with every number checked against NASA's own data, in under a
minute."
